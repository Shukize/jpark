How to use the Site Editor

Everything here updates the live website right away. Use the tabs below to edit words, swap photos, change colours, show or hide sections, and review who changed what. Nothing is permanent — every field has its own Reset.

  1. Pick a tabWebsite text, Photos & videos, Colours, Sections, and Previous edits each live on their own tab so the page stays tidy.
  2. Edit text in any languageOn the Text tab, choose the language you are editing, then search or browse the grouped sections. Each group shows a small picture of where it appears — click it to open that spot on the live site.
  3. Auto-translationWhen you change a piece of text it saves itself and is automatically translated into the other four languages so every language stays in sync. You can still fine-tune any language by hand.
  4. Manage photos & videosOn the Photos & videos tab, open any section to add, replace, reorder, or remove photos and videos. Upload a photo from your computer or paste an image or video link.
  5. Colours & sectionsRecolour the whole site on the Colours tab, and show/hide sections or post an announcement banner on the Sections tab.
  6. See the historyThe Previous edits tab lists every change with who made it and when, so the team can track what has been updated.
  7. Rates are live, not cosmeticUnlike every other tab, changes on the Rates tab affect real guest charges the moment you click Save — there is no draft step and no "Undo all my edits" safety net for rates.
  8. Live Chat answersOn the Live Chat tab, reword what the chat assistant tells guests, change the times and details inside each answer, pick which words trigger it, or add a new answer of your own. Type once in your language and it is translated into the other four. Click Save Live Chat to publish — it reaches every guest device.
Good to know
  • Open the website in a second tab and keep it beside this one — your edits appear there live as you save.
  • A field left exactly as the original stores nothing — only your real changes are kept.
  • Auto-translation uses an online service; if it is unavailable the other languages keep their current text and you can edit them by hand.
  • The Rates tab is the one exception to "nothing is permanent" — double-check a number before saving, since it takes effect on the very next booking.

Maintenance Mode

When enabled, guests see a maintenance notice instead of the live site. Staff can still sign in here.

Require prepayment (busy periods)

When enabled, guests can't choose "pay at check-in" — every new booking must pay online now and is non-refundable on no-show or cancellation. Turn it on for holidays and peak periods, off when the hotel is quiet.

Note: online payment isn't live yet, so this has no effect until the hotel's card / PromptPay payments are switched on.
