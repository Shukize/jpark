Welcome to J Park

Where Japan meets the Thai tropics

Set in Na Pa, Chonburi, J Park Hotel blends the calm of a Japanese ryokan with the warmth of Thai hospitality. Spacious, apartment-style suites make it as comfortable for a single night as for a long stay.

Soak in our traditional onsen, dine on authentic Japanese cuisine at Tsubaki, swim beneath the palms, then unwind at the brand-new Midnight Coffee Club. Just minutes from Amata Nakorn and within easy reach of Bangsaen Beach and Bangkok.

Bathe & restore

Japanese Onsen

Rich in minerals and natural extracts, our Futamata Onsen recreates the original hot-spring infusions of Japan — a serene soak to soften the skin, ease the body and quiet the mind. Separate baths for men and women.

Signature mineral baths

Four celebrated Japanese hot-spring infusions, available at the counter — we'll be delighted to prepare one for you.

  • GeroRelaxes the body, refines the skin and leaves it feeling softer.
  • HakoneEases restless sleep, soothes minor wounds and supports healthy circulation.
  • NoboribetsuRelieves aching muscles, fatigue and stiff shoulders while improving circulation.
  • KusatsuCalms dry, cracked or irritated skin and nourishes it for a healthy glow.
